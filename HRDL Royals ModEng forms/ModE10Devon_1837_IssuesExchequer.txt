[1413]

20 th May. — To William Godezer, a citizen and coppersmith, of London. In money paid to his own hands, in advance for newly devising and making an image in likeness of the mother of the present Lord the King [Henry V], ornamented with divers arms of the Kings of England, and placed over the tomb of the said King’s mother [Mary de Bohun], within the King’s college at Leicester, where the mother of the aforesaid Lord the King is buried and entombed, — 43l.
