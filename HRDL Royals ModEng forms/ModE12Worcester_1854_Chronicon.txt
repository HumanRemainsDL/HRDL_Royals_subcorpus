While the king [Edward the Elder] was thus employed, that is to say, on the nineteenth of the calends of July, his sister, Ethelfleda, lady of the Mercians, a woman of incomparable prudence, and eminent for her just and virtuous life, died, eight years after the sole government of the Mercians fell to her, during which she had ruled them with firmness and equity. She left Elfwina, her only daughter by Ethelred the sub-king, heiress to her territories: her body was carried to Gloucester and honourably buried in the church of St. Peter.
