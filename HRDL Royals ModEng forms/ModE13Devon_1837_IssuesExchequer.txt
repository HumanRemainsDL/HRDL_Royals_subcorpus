[1395]

24th April. — To Peter Merk and James Monald. In money paid to them by the hands of the same Peter, in discharge of 61. 19s. which the Lord the King commanded to be paid them for so much money by them paid for costs incurred about the carriage and portage of a gold cup and a gold ring set with a ruby; also a Book of the Miracles of Edward, late King of England, whose body was buried at the town of Gloucester, — to wit, from London to the city of Florence, to make a present of the same to our most holy Father Pope Urban, on behalf of the Lord the King. By writ, &c., — 6l. 19s.
