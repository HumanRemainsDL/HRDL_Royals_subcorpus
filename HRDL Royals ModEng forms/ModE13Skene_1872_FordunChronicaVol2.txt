So the king [Constantine] was left on the field by a great part of his army, and beset by the enemy and slain. When the enemy, after their victory there, had retreated to their ships, the routed inhabitants returned; and, after searching the field, they found the kings body, and bore it with deep wailing to the island of Iona, where it was enshrined, with great honours, in his fathers bosom.
