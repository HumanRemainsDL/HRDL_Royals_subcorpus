Ivar  the  Legless  was  king  in  England  for  a  long time.  He  had  no  child,  because  [so  it  is  said]  he  had  not  the  desire  nor the  faculty  for  that :  but  he  had  no  lack  of  understanding  or  ferocity.  He died  of  old  age  in  England,  and  was  buried  there.  Then  were  all Lodbrok's  sons  dead.
