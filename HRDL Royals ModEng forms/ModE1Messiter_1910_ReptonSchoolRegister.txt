[1779]
Up to 1768 the Master [William Prior] had always lived in [Repton] Priory (North end), and the 1st Usher in the Little Priory (South end), it was in digging a vault for Dr. Prior inside the Chancel of the Church that the Sexton fell through into the long-forgotten crypt, which had been closed up in 1670, at the end of the Thacker suit, to prevent the boys from using it.
