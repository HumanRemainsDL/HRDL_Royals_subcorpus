Aud was a tall stately dame. When she was outworn with age, she invited to her her kinsmen and kinsmen-in-law, and prepared a rich banquet. And when the banquet had gone on for three nights, she chose gifts for her friends, and gave them good counsel. She said that the banquet should go on for other three nights; she said that this should be her funeral feast. The night after, she died: and she was buried on the ebb-shore, as she had said before, because she wished not to lie in unconsecrated ground, since she was baptized. After that the faith of her relatives deteriorated.
