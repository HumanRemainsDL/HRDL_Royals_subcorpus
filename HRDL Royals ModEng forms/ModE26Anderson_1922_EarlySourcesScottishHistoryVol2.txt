Olaf went to the court of the king of Norway. But before he arrived there, Hakon the king of Norway had appointed a certain noble man of royal lineage, called Ospak Ogmund's son, king over the Hebridean islands; and had called his name Hakon. And this Hakon came with Olaf and Godfrey Dond, Reginald's son, and with a great company of Norwegians, to the Hebridean islands. And when they had come to the island that is called Bute, and wished to storm the castle that is in it, the aforesaid [Ospakr-Hakon] was struck down with a stone, and perished; and he was buried in the island of Iona.
