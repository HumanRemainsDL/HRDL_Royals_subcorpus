From a very interesting paper in the Proceedings of the Scottish Antiquaries, vol. x. p. 403, by Joseph Anderson, LL.D., we learn that the Princess [Margaret of Norway] died (but was not buried) in Orkney - that Bishop Narwe of Bergen, in whose arms she died, brought her body back to Norway, and the King her father, after satisfying himself that it was his daughter's corpse, caused her to be buried beside her mother on the north side of the choir of Bergen cathedral.
