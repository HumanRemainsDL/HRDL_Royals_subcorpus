[1307 30 June] Letter  to  Monsire  Hugh  de  Neville  from  his  vallet  Euweyn, mentioning  that  the  Archbishop  is  well — that  the  late  K. Edward [I]’s body lay at  Richmond  on  Saturday  (29th) — the  Archbishop  this Sunday  leaves  Scroby  to  attend  it — that  the  ‘ Patriark,’  the  Earl  of Lincoln,  and  other  magnates,  did  homage  at  Carlisle  to  the  new  K., and  have  accompanied him  into  Scotland  against  the  enemy.
