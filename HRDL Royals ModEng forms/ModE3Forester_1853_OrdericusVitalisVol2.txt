[1067]

This abbey has always been better known as Sanctus Martinus de Bello, or Battle Abbey. William determined, notwithstanding the opposition of the monks, to build it on the field of battle, so much, that the high altar was placed on the spot where the body of Harold was found after the battle, as some say, but as others, where the royal standard was taken. Part of the church was built of Caen stone, until a quarry was discovered in the neighbourhood.
