In the year 1338 died Andrew of Moray, the warden of Scotland, and was buried at Rosemary's; but his bones were afterwards brought down to Dunfermline, and entombed before the altar of the Blessed Virgin, in the monastery of that place. He did a good deal for his countries freedom; and assaulted and destroyed all the castles and strongholds held by the English about the water of Forth, except Cuper and Perth. But all the country he marched through, in his wars, he reduced to such desolation and distress, that more perished afterwards, through starvation and want, than the sword devoured in tiny over. He was two years and a half guardian of Scotland.
