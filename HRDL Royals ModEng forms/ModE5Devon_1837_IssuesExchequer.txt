[1359]

4th February. — To John Galeys, in money paid to him in discharge of 10l. which the Lord the King commanded to be paid him in recompense for the loss which the said John sustained for the accommodation of his houses at the Milehend during the time the body of Isabella, late Queen of England, the King’s mother, remained there with the King and his household. By writ of great seal, &c. — 10l. 17s
